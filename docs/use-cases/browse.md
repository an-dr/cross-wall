# Browse

### UC-BROWSE-01: See photos with no setup

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: Find a wallpaper without knowing what to search for.
- **Flow**: Open the app, optionally pick another source, and scroll, pressing Load more for further photos.
- **Success**: A thumbnail grid fills in while the window stays responsive. No key or account is needed, except for Pexels. Photos credit their photographer where the source names one, and the screen links to the source. Network failures and rate limits show a message.

### UC-BROWSE-02: Search photos

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: Find photos of a subject in the shape of my screen.
- **Flow**: Type a query, choose an orientation, and press Search.
- **Success**: The grid shows the matching photos, or says there are none. Lorem Picsum has no search, and its search box says so.

### UC-BROWSE-03: Preview a photo

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: Judge a photo before keeping it.
- **Flow**: Click a thumbnail.
- **Success**: A larger view shows the photo, its creator, size, and licence where known, with Like and Apply buttons.
