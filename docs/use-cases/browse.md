# Browse

### UC-BROWSE-01: See curated photos

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: Find a wallpaper without knowing what to search for.
- **Flow**: Open Browse and scroll, pressing Load more for further photos.
- **Success**: A thumbnail grid fills in while the window stays responsive. Each photo credits its photographer and the screen links to Pexels. Network failures and rate limits show a message.

### UC-BROWSE-02: Search photos

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: Find photos of a subject in the shape of my screen.
- **Flow**: Type a query, choose an orientation, and press Search.
- **Success**: The grid shows the matching photos, or says there are none.

### UC-BROWSE-03: Preview a photo

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: Judge a photo before keeping it.
- **Flow**: Click a thumbnail.
- **Success**: A larger view shows the photo, its photographer, and its size, with Like and Apply buttons.
