# Library

### UC-LIBRARY-01: Like a photo

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: Keep a photo for good, on all my computers.
- **Flow**: Press Like on a photo.
- **Success**: The photo appears under Liked once its download has finished, and opens there with no network.

### UC-LIBRARY-02: Unlike a photo

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: Stop keeping a photo.
- **Flow**: Press Unlike on a photo.
- **Success**: It leaves Liked here and, once the folder has synced, on my other computers.

### UC-LIBRARY-03: Receive likes from another computer

- **Actor**: Wallpaper user.
- **Goal**: See what I liked elsewhere.
- **Flow**: Like a photo on another computer and let the folder sync.
- **Success**: The photo appears under Liked without restarting the app. Liking different photos at once on two computers keeps them all.
